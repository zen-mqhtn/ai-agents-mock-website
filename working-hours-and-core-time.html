Working hours & core time

Owned by People Operations · Last updated 12 September 2026 · Applies in the office and when working remotely

Acme uses flexible working hours. You decide when you work, within the following rules. They are the same whether you work in the office or remotely.

What are the core hours?

Core hours are 10:00–15:00 (local time, Monday to Friday). During core hours, please be available for meetings and messages.

Flexible working window

RuleDetails
Flexible working windowWork any time between 07:00 and 20:00. Outside this window, don't schedule meetings.
Standard week40 hours for full-time employees
Maximum per day10 hours, including when working remotely
BreaksAt least 30 minutes after 6 hours of work, at least 45 minutes after 9 hours
Rest periodAt least 11 hours between two working days

Meeting-free Friday afternoons

No internal meetings on Fridays after 13:00. Use the time for focused work.

Distributed teams

If your team works across time zones, agree on at least 2 hours of overlap per day within everyone's core hours. Keep your Slack status and working hours in Google Calendar up to date.

Overtime

Overtime must be agreed with your manager in advance and is compensated with time off in lieu within 3 months.
