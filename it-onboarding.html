IT onboarding checklist

Owned by IT Service Desk · Last updated 1 August 2026

Welcome to Acme! Work through this checklist on your first day. It takes about an hour.

Day 1 checklist

  1. Pick up your laptop at the IT desk or open the package sent to your home.
  2. Sign in with the temporary password from your welcome email and set a new password.
  3. Set up MFA with the Acme Authenticator app on your phone.
  4. Install the password manager extension in your browser.
  5. Connect the AcmeConnect VPN once to check that it works.
  6. Join Slack and the channels below.

Important Slack channels

ChannelUse it for
#it-helpQuestions about laptops, accounts and software
#it-securityReporting security incidents and phishing
#acme-announcementsCompany news
#people-helpHR questions (or just ask the Acme Assistant)

Software

Standard software is pre-installed. Additional software can be installed from the Self Service app. Licences for paid tools are requested through the Acme Assistant.

Printing

Follow-me printing is available on every floor. Hold your badge to the printer to release your print job.
