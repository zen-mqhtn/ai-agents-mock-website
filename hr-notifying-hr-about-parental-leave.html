HR process: Notifying HR about parental leave

Owned by the People Operations team · Last updated 3 November 2026

You are expecting a child or are about to adopt? Congratulations! This page explains when and how to notify HR so that your leave, pay and benefits are set up in time.

When should I notify HR?

Notify HR at least 15 weeks before your expected due date. For adoption, notify HR as soon as the placement date is confirmed.

Earlier is always welcome. HR treats your notification confidentially and won't inform your team.

How do I notify HR?

  1. Open PeopleHub > Life events > "Expecting a child", or ask the Acme Assistant to start the process for you.
  2. Enter your expected due date (or placement date) and when you'd like your leave to start.
  3. Upload a confirmation of the expected due date from your doctor or midwife (in the UK, the MATB1 form).
  4. Submit the form. Your manager is informed automatically once you submit. We recommend telling your manager yourself first.

What happens next?

WhenWhat happens
Within 5 business daysYour HR Business Partner contacts you to plan your leave and answer your questions.
Before your leave startsYou receive a written confirmation of your leave dates and pay.
4 weeks before you returnReturn-to-work conversation with your manager.
Working in Germany? In addition to this notification, you must request your Elternzeit in writing within the legal deadline. See the Betriebsvereinbarung Elternzeit for details.
