Reception and badges

Every office has a staffed reception, open Monday to Friday, 08:00–18:00.
  • New or replacement badge: pick it up at your office reception. Bring a photo ID and hand in your old badge if you have one.
  • Forgot your badge? Reception gives you a day pass.
  • Visitors must be registered in the visitor system at least 24 hours in advance.

Desks and rooms

All offices use desk sharing. Book your desk and meeting rooms in the Acme Workplace app. Team anchor days are pre-booked for each team.
